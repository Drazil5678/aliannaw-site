About

Alianna Waggoner is a singer, actress, and dancer based in Tampa Bay and New York City. Originally from Maine, she is a graduate of Circle in the Square Theatre School on Broadway, where she completed her training in Musical Theatre.

Alianna is a member of Dot in the Box Theater Company, a company associated with Circle in the Square. Her performance experience spans musical theatre, Shakespeare, contemporary theatre, and new works.

With a strong foundation in acting, voice, dance, and movement, Alianna brings professionalism, versatility, and emotional authenticity to her work. She is passionate about storytelling and creating meaningful connections with audiences.
