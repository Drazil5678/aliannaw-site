About

Alianna Waggoner is a singer, actor, and dancer based in Tampa Bay and New York City.

A graduate of the Circle in the Square Theatre School on Broadway, Alianna trained in musical theatre with a focus on acting, voice, movement, and performance.

Her performance experience spans Shakespeare, contemporary political theatre, new works, and musical theatre. She is currently a member of Dot in the Box Theater Company, affiliated with Circle in the Square.

Alianna brings a combination of strong vocal ability, physicality, emotional authenticity, and professionalism to her work, with a particular interest in storytelling that creates a meaningful connection with an audience.
