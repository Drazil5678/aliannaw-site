About

Alianna Waggoner is a singer, actor, and dancer originally from Maine and now working between Tampa Bay and New York City. A graduate of the Musical Theatre program at Eckerd College and Circle in the Square Theatre School, she earned her BFA in Musical Theatre with a minor in Italian. Her performance experience spans Shakespeare, contemporary political theatre, new works, and musical theatre. Alianna is a member of Dot in the Box Theater Company in association with Circle in the Square, where she continues to develop her work as a professional performer. Known for her strong vocal ability, emotional authenticity, versatility, and collaborative spirit, Alianna brings depth, intelligence, and a strong commitment to storytelling to every project.
